Red 6 AR website

Web design

A minimal, modern website for an augmented reality company that builds hardware and software for fighter pilot training.

The problem

Red 6 needed a minimal, modern website that would act as a visual gateway into its work. The challenge was to deliver a seamless experience while showcasing striking imagery of its technology.

The solution

I worked with Red 6 to build a site that pairs a sleek look with straightforward navigation. It reflects the company's forward-thinking culture and tells a clear story about its augmented reality products, turning complex technology into something visually striking and easy to follow.
