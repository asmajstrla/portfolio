Hawkers branding

Brand identity and creative direction

A brand system for a fast-growing Asian street food restaurant group, built to feel as loud, warm and hand-made as the food, and to scale from 4 to 15 locations across the country.

The brief

Hawkers started as a single Orlando restaurant serving the dishes its founders grew up with at street stalls across Asia. As the group expanded, the brand had to travel to new cities without losing the energy of the original: every location needed to feel local and lived-in, not like a chain.

The challenge was to build one system flexible enough for menus, packaging, apparel, signage and full-wall murals, while giving each new restaurant its own personality.

My role

I joined as senior art director and later became creative director, leading an in-house team of designers, photographers and videographers.

I owned the brand end to end: the 40-page brand guidelines, the menu suite, takeout packaging, uniforms and merch, neon signage and seasonal campaigns. I also traveled to new locations to design and paint the murals myself.

The system

The tiger mascot is the anchor. It shows up as a full illustration on murals and Lunar New Year pieces, simplified on packaging, and as a single-line mark on pins and caps. Around it sits a tight palette of tiger orange, deep green and cream, a bold sans for headlines and hand lettering for moments that need personality.

Every year the zodiac animal becomes a new campaign, which keeps the brand fresh while reinforcing the tiger. The work earned Central Florida Art Director of the Year and a string of ADDY awards.
