The brief

At both Hawkers and UCF, a big part of the job was turning walls into part of the brand. At Hawkers, every restaurant needed to feel like stepping onto a street in Asia. At UCF, campus spaces and public places across Orlando needed to carry the university brand at a large scale.

My role

I designed most of these pieces myself and drew them by hand before they went up on the walls. At Hawkers I also painted multiple murals on location by hand. At UCF I designed wall wraps, wayfinding and out-of-home campaigns.

The system

  • Layered street posters at Hawkers, hand-installed on the walls to recreate the way ads stack on top of each other on the streets of Asia
  • Custom neon signs for Hawkers restaurants
  • Hand-painted murals, done on location
  • Wall wraps at the UCF Downtown campus and Millican Hall
  • Wayfinding for UCF
  • Wall wraps at Orlando International Airport (MCO)
  • Bus wraps and billboards
