The idea

Hawkers wanted more people through the door on weekend mornings. Instead of just adding a brunch menu, we gave brunch its own name, look and personality: a small brand that lives inside the bigger one.

My role

I came up with the concept and led it from first sketch to launch: the name and look, the menu, the photoshoot and everything people saw online.

What we made

  • Printed brunch menus
  • Brunch pages on the Hawkers website
  • Augmented reality filters for Instagram
  • An interactive quiz to find your brunch order
  • Spotify playlists to set the mood
  • Email campaigns to bring guests in

The results

In just six weeks, the launch reached millions and paid for itself many times over.
