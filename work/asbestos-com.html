Asbestos.com homepage

A homepage redesign to build trust, simplify navigation and use real imagery so visitors can picture themselves using the service.

Designed for
Asbestos.com
Date
February 2024
Role
UX and web design
Result
Creating frictionless conversions

The problem

Asbestos.com is a deep, knowledge-based resource, similar to WebMD or Wikipedia. For most of its audience, people 65 and older, the sheer amount of information can be overwhelming, and the site struggled to guide visitors to the content they needed.

The solution

  • Clear trust signals near the top to build confidence quickly.
  • A more visible call to action for the free guide, the site's highest-converting element.
  • Entry points for patients, caregivers and medical professionals, so each group finds relevant content fast.
  • Better search, for information that doesn't live in the top navigation.
